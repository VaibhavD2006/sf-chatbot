# Example Questions for Solo Founders Chat

These questions are drawn from real episodes in the transcript library.

---

## Podcast Content Questions

### Co-founding & Going Solo
- What did Charles Hudson say about co-founder breakup rates?
- Why does Julian Weisser think the co-founder model is overrated?
- What's the "denominator delusion" Julian mentioned?
- How did David Phillips end up founding Fondo alone after multiple co-founder splits?
- What did Florent Crivello say about the risks of co-founder breakups?

### Finding Product-Market Fit
- How many pivots did Rahul Sonwalkar go through before finding PMF with Julius?
- What did John Glasgow say about his two-year search for product-market fit?
- What was Dhravya Shah's experience with viral validation vs. real product-market fit?
- How did Yasser Elsaid ride the AI wave to $9M ARR at Chatbase?

### Hiring & Team Building
- What did Michael Grinich say about how hiring strategy changes before and after PMF?
- What does Ben Broca mean by AI teammates as a proxy for a full team?
- How does Paul Klein IV think about being a "five-tool founder"?
- What did Michael Grinich say about stage-appropriate teams?

### Conviction & Founder Mindset
- Why did Jimmy Douglas leave Tesla to start Plug?
- What did Yasser Elsaid say about independent sports shaping the solo founder mindset?
- How does John Glasgow describe what it takes to have deep conviction in a problem?
- What did Julian Weisser mean by "companies die from lost hope, not money"?

### Building with AI
- How is Claire Vo using AI agents at ChatPRD?
- What does Ben Broca think about AI as a creative director?
- How did Francis Davidson build Odessia in 5 months using coding agents?
- What is Eugenia Kuyda's view on conversation as art in AI companions?
- What does Dhravya Shah think about building for love vs. building for VCs?

### Bootstrapping & Funding
- How did Yasser Elsaid grow Chatbase to $9M ARR without raising money?
- What did Dhravya Shah say about avoiding investor pressure by going open source first?
- What did David Phillips say about managing investor relationships through a pivot?

### Specific Guest Deep-Dives
- Summarize everything Eugenia Kuyda said about building Replika.
- What are Minn Kim's main insights about scaling legal services with AI?
- What did Thais Castello Branco say about building community-driven products?
- What was Sunil's path from journalism to civic tech with Hamlet?
- What did Paul Bakaus say about the "40-year-old founder" advantage?
- What is Yakko Majuri's view on internal tools infrastructure?
